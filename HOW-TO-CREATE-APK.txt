       SANGEET LOCAL - HOW TO GET YOUR ANDROID APK / APP
===================================================================

Your music app is now 100% LIVE worldwide at:
👉 https://prg-git.github.io/sangeet-local/

Here are the 2 fastest ways to get your APK / install on your phone:

-------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 1: 1-MINUTE FREE APK DOWNLOAD (Via PWABuilder)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In your PC or phone browser, go to:
      https://www.pwabuilder.com

2. Paste your live link:
      https://prg-git.github.io/sangeet-local/
   and click "Start".

3. Click "Package for Stores" -> Select "Android".
4. Click "Download APK Package".
5. Send the downloaded .apk file to your Android phone and install!

-------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 2: 1-TAP DIRECT PHONE INSTALL (No APK Download Needed)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open this link on your Android phone in Google Chrome:
      https://prg-git.github.io/sangeet-local/
2. Tap the Chrome 3-dots menu (top right).
3. Tap "Install App" (or "Add to Home screen").
4. Sangeet Local will install with its custom purple music icon on your
   phone's home screen with full-screen playback and background audio!

-------------------------------------------------------------------
EASY SHORT WEB ADDRESS OPTIONS:
-------------------------------------------------------------------
If you want an easy link to share with friends:
1. Short link: Go to tinyurl.com or bitly.com and create:
   tinyurl.com/sangeet-app  ==> redirects to your GitHub link!

2. Custom Domain (.com, .in):
   In GitHub Settings -> Pages -> Custom Domain, you can connect your
   own domain (like sangeet.in or pinku.live) for free!
