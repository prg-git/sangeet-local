===================================================================
       SANGEET LOCAL - HOW TO GET YOUR ANDROID APK / APP
===================================================================

You have 3 easy ways to convert Sangeet Local into an Android App / APK:

-------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 1: 1-CLICK FREE APK BUILDER (Fastest - No Coding Needed)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. On your PC, double-click:
      start-mobile-internet.bat
   This starts the Cloudflare tunnel and copies your secure HTTPS link
   (e.g., https://your-music-link.trycloudflare.com).

2. Open your PC or phone browser and visit Microsoft & Google's official PWA tool:
      https://www.pwabuilder.com

3. Paste your Cloudflare HTTPS link into the box and click "Start".
4. Click "Package for Stores" -> Select "Android" -> Click "Download APK".
5. Send the downloaded .apk to your phone (via WhatsApp, Telegram, or USB) and tap "Install"!

-------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 2: DIRECT 1-TAP INSTALL (No APK Download Required)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Modern Android phones can install Sangeet Local directly like a native app:
1. Open your Sangeet link in Google Chrome on your phone:
   - On same Wi-Fi: http://192.168.31.41:8080
   - On mobile data: Your Cloudflare HTTPS link
2. Tap the Chrome 3-dots menu (top right).
3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install App".
4. Sangeet Local will now appear on your phone home screen with its custom
   music icon and runs in full-screen standalone mode with background audio!

-------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 3: COMPILE NATIVE APK WITH ANDROID STUDIO
-------------------------------------------------------------------
A complete, pre-configured Android Studio project is ready in:
   c:\Project\Latetst\android-apk-project\

1. Open Android Studio.
2. Click "Open" and select the folder:
      c:\Project\Latetst\android-apk-project
3. Wait for Gradle sync to complete.
4. Go to top menu: Build -> Build Bundle(s) / APK(s) -> Build APK(s).
5. Android Studio will generate the signed/unsigned .apk in:
      app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
6. Copy that .apk to any Android phone and install!

Tip: When opening the app on your phone, you can long-press anywhere on the screen
to enter a new Wi-Fi IP or Cloudflare tunnel link at any time!
