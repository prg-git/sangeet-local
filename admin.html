How to Publish Updates from PC:

  1. On your PC, open the project folder: C:\Project\Latetst
  2. Double-click: upload-to-github.bat
  3. Press ENTER. All updated songs and 3-language banners will be pushed to GitHub!
  4. Within 30–60 seconds, your mobile app at prg-git.github.io/sangeet-local will be updated for all listeners!
